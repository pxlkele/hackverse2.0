Aap aaj FSSAI Basic Registration ke liye eligible hain - food bechne ke liye legal permission. Aap aaj PMSBY ke liye bhi eligible hain - accidental death ya disability ke liye 2 lakh ka cover, sirf Rs 20 per year ka premium auto-debited bank ya post office account se. Aap aaj PMJJBY ke liye bhi eligible hain - life cover Rs 2 lakh ke liye kisi bhi cause se death hone par, premium Rs 330 per year auto-debited savings account se. PM SVANidhi ke liye aap tak pahunch sakta hai up to 15000 rupees. Aap bas do step door hain - free mein, around 7 days mein. Aapka pehla step: kisi bhi payments app linked bank account se UPI ID create karein - BHIM, PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm jaise kahin bhi.
