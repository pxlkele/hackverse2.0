Aap aaj PMSBY ke liye eligible hain - accidental death ya disability ke liye Rs 2 lakh ka cover, premium Rs 20 saal mein auto-debited bank ya post office account se. Aap aaj PMJJBY ke liye bhi eligible hain - kisi bhi cause se marne par Rs 2 lakh ki life cover, premium Rs 330 saal mein auto-debited savings account se. Aap aaj PM SVANidhi ke liye bhi eligible hain - collateral-free working capital loan teen tranche mein.
